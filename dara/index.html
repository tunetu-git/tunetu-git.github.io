What Dara Automat is

Dara Automat is an internal tool that manages DARA's own official YouTube channel, Dara_Official. It:

  • uploads short videos made from DARA's own music videos,
  • keeps titles, descriptions, tags and playlists of the channel up to date in Ukrainian and English,
  • reads the statistics of this one channel to plan future posts.

The tool is not offered to the public. Only the owner of the channel signs in to it once; no other users sign in or provide data.
