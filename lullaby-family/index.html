Lullaby Family Automat

Lullaby Family Automat is an internal tool that manages the official Lullaby Family YouTube channel (@lullabyfamilyworld). It:

  • uploads short videos set to Lullaby Family's own music,
  • keeps titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails and playlists of the channel up to date,
  • reads the statistics of this one channel to plan future videos.

The tool is not offered to the public. Only the owner of the channel signs in to it once; no other users sign in or provide data.
